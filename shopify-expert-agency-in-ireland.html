How much does a custom Shopify store cost in Ireland?+

It depends on the size of your catalogue and how custom the design is. A focused store with a strong theme build costs far less than a fully bespoke design with custom apps. Tell us about your products and we'll give you a clear, fixed quote — no hourly surprises.

Do you only work with businesses in Dublin and Galway?+

No — we build Shopify stores for clients across Dublin, Galway, Cork and anywhere in Ireland, and we market them nationwide. Most of the work happens online; calls happen whenever suits you.

Will my store rank on Google?+

Every store we build ships with SEO fundamentals: clean URLs, fast mobile pages, structured data and keyword-mapped product pages. For competitive keywords, our monthly SEO services build rankings steadily after launch.

Can you take over my existing Shopify store?+

Yes. We audit what you have, fix what's holding it back, and grow it with SEO and marketing — you don't need to start from scratch.
