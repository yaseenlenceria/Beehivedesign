An open web, for everyone.

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Our commitment

Beehive Design wants everyone to be able to use this website — including people who browse with screen readers, keyboard only, magnification, or reduced-motion settings. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA.

What we’ve done

  • Semantic HTML with a clear heading structure and landmarks on every page
  • Keyboard-operable navigation, menus and accordions, with visible focus states
  • Text colour contrast tested against WCAG AA contrast ratios
  • Alt text on meaningful images; decorative graphics hidden from assistive technology
  • All animations disabled automatically for users with reduced-motion enabled
  • Form fields with real labels, required-field marking and status messages announced to screen readers
  • Responsive layout that works at 200% zoom and on small screens

Known limitations

Some older blog articles imported from our previous website may contain images without complete alt text. We are reviewing these and updating them as we find them.

Feedback

If anything on this site creates a barrier for you, tell us and we will fix it. Email info@beehivedesign.ie or call +353 85 179 1491. We aim to respond within five working days.

Enforcement

This statement was prepared on 24 September 2026 and is reviewed regularly. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can consult the W3C Web Accessibility Initiative resources for further guidance.
