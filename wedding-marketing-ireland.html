Common questions

Answered honestly

How do wedding venues get found on Google in Ireland?+

Three things move the needle: ranking for county and venue-type searches with dedicated, well-built pages; a complete Google Business Profile with steady reviews for map results; and listings across wedding directories that reinforce your credibility. We manage all three.

When should wedding businesses start SEO for next season?+

Couples start searching 9–18 months before their date — so visibility work for next summer needs to start now, ideally before the autumn engagement peak. SEO compounds; the earlier you start, the more of the season you capture.

Do you only do SEO for wedding businesses?+

No — Beehive Design is a full-service SEO agency. But the wedding industry is one of our specialities: we understand the booking journey, the seasonality and the search behaviour unique to Irish couples.
