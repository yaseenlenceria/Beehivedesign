What Is SEO? 🤔

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization.

It’s the process of improving your website so it appears higher on Google when people search for your services.Example:

If someone searches “SEO agency Dublin” or “SEO services Ireland”, SEO helps your website show up on page 1 instead of page 5.More visibility = more clicks = more customers 💰

What Is Technical SEO? ⚙️

Technical SEO makes sure your website is fast, crawlable, and error-free.It includes:

• Site speed optimization

• Mobile responsiveness

• Core Web Vitals

• Indexing & crawl fixes

• Structured dataWithout technical SEO, even great content won’t rank.

Frequently asked questions

What is SEO in simple terms?+

SEO (search engine optimisation) is making your website show up when people search Google for what you sell. It covers technical health, the words on your pages, and your reputation across the web. Done well, it brings customers to your site without paying for ads.

Why does my business need SEO?+

Your customers search online before they buy, call or visit. If your site doesn't appear for those searches, your competitors get the call. SEO turns your website from a brochure into a consistent source of leads.

How long does SEO take to show results?+

Early improvements often appear within weeks, but solid, lasting rankings usually build over 3–6 months. It depends on your market's competition and the current state of your website.
