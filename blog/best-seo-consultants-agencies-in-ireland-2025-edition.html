How Do I Know If My SEO Company Is Doing a Good Job?

Check if your SEO provider delivers:

  • Monthly reports (traffic, rankings, leads)
  • Regular blog or content updates

You can use tools like Ahrefs, SEMrush, or Ubersuggest to validate results.

How much do SEO agencies in Ireland charge?+

Most Irish SEO agencies work on monthly retainers. Small-business packages typically start from around €300 per month and scale with competition, goals and scope. Always ask what deliverables are included before comparing prices.

How do I choose the right SEO agency?+

Look for three things: transparent reporting focused on leads and sales (not vanity metrics), white-hat techniques that comply with Google's guidelines, and case studies in your industry or market. A free audit is a low-risk way to test an agency's thinking.
