10 Best Wedding Planners in Ireland for 2026

17 August 2026· 5 min read· by Beehive Design — SEO Agency Ireland

Planning a wedding should be exciting, not overwhelming. But between finding the right venue, managing suppliers, creating timelines and making sure everything comes together on the day, there is a lot to organise.That is exactly where an experienced wedding planner can make all the difference.To help you begin your search, we have put together our selection of 10 of the best wedding planners in Ireland for 2026, looking at the type of planning support offered, approach, style and suitability for different couples.

1. Weddings with Erica

Best for: Personal, stress free wedding planning and coordinationIf you want to actually enjoy your engagement and wedding day rather than spending it worrying about schedules, suppliers and small details, Weddings with Erica is our top recommendation.Erica takes a personal and supportive approach to wedding planning, helping couples turn their ideas into a beautifully organised celebration while taking much of the pressure out of the process.What makes Weddings with Erica particularly appealing is the focus on allowing couples to remain present throughout their wedding experience.Instead of feeling as though your wedding has become another project to manage, you have someone helping keep everything organised and moving in the right direction.


Why we recommend Weddings with Erica

Erica's approach is ideal for couples who want professional organisation without losing the personal feeling of their wedding.From planning support to wedding day coordination, the aim is simple: you enjoy the moment while Erica handles the rest.This makes Weddings with Erica particularly suitable for couples who have a clear idea of the wedding they want but need an experienced person to bring all of the moving pieces together. Our pick: Best overall wedding planner in Ireland for couples looking for a personal and hands on planning experience.[Visit Weddings with Erica]

2. Tara Fay Events

Best for: Luxury and large scale weddingsTara Fay is one of the most established names in the Irish wedding industry and is particularly well known for sophisticated luxury celebrations.Her team has experience producing detailed weddings and events where design, logistics and guest experience all need to work seamlessly together.For couples planning a large luxury wedding or an elaborate multi day celebration, Tara Fay Events is certainly a name worth considering.

3. House of Hannah

Best for: Stylish and design led weddingsHouse of Hannah is known for beautifully designed celebrations with a polished, editorial feel.Their work is particularly suited to couples who care deeply about aesthetics and want every part of their wedding to feel connected, from stationery and tablescapes to flowers and the overall atmosphere.

4. Dream Irish Wedding

Best for: Destination weddings in IrelandDream Irish Wedding is an established choice for couples planning a destination wedding in Ireland.The company has experience working with luxury Irish venues and helping couples organise celebrations when they may not be based in Ireland themselves.This can be especially valuable when coordinating venues and suppliers from another country.

5. Perfect Details

Best for: Detailed planning and creative weddingsAs the name suggests, Perfect Details places considerable emphasis on the smaller elements that bring an entire wedding together.For couples looking for a combination of organisation, creativity and wedding styling, they are another established Irish wedding planning option worth exploring.

6. Frog Prince Weddings & Events

Best for: Creative event styling and wedding productionFrog Prince has extensive experience within Ireland's wedding and events industry.The company combines planning with styling and event production, making it particularly relevant for couples looking for a wedding with a strong visual identity.

7. Love & Gatherings

Best for: Warm and personal celebrationsLove & Gatherings focuses on creating celebrations that feel personal to the couple.Rather than approaching every wedding in exactly the same way, the emphasis is on creating an experience that reflects the people getting married.This can make them a good option for couples looking for something intimate and individual.

8. Infusion Wedding Concepts

Best for: Bespoke wedding planningInfusion Wedding Concepts provides personalised wedding planning and design.Their approach makes them worth considering for couples who want professional guidance while still maintaining their own vision and personality throughout the celebration.

9. Waterlily Weddings

Best for: Destination wedding planningWaterlily Weddings is another recognised name for couples considering Ireland as their destination wedding location.Destination weddings involve additional logistical challenges, particularly when couples and guests are travelling internationally, so specialist experience can be extremely valuable.

10. MartinaO

Best for: Flexible wedding planning servicesMartinaO offers a broad range of wedding planning services, including full wedding planning, wedding coordination, consultations, styling and design.That flexibility makes the service particularly useful for couples who may not require complete wedding planning but still want professional help with specific parts of the process.

How to Choose the Best Wedding Planner in Ireland

The best wedding planner for your celebration is not necessarily the person organising the biggest or most expensive weddings.You need someone who understands your wedding.Before choosing your planner, consider:

  • The level of planning support you actually need
  • Whether you connect with their personality
  • Their communication style
  • Experience with your type of wedding
  • Wedding day coordination
  • Supplier and venue knowledge
  • Their approach to budgets and timelines
  • Whether their planning style matches your vision

Your planner will be involved in some of the most important decisions surrounding your wedding, so feeling comfortable with them matters just as much as their portfolio.

Do You Really Need a Wedding Planner?

Not every couple needs full wedding planning.If you enjoy organising everything yourself, you may simply need professional support closer to the wedding.This is where wedding day coordination can be incredibly valuable.You have already put months of work into creating your wedding. On the actual day, you should not be checking whether suppliers have arrived, answering logistical questions or watching the clock.Having someone else managing those details allows you to concentrate on what the day is actually about.

I'll Handle the Rest.That is ultimately what great wedding planning should achieve.Your wedding should feel like your wedding, not an event you have been hired to manage.Whether you need support during the planning process or someone dependable coordinating everything on the day, Erica offers couples the opportunity to step away from the logistics and actually experience the celebration they have spent so long planning.
