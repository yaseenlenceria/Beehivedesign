What is AI search optimisation?

AI search optimisation — also called Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) or LLM SEO — is the practice of making your business the one that AI assistants recommend. When someone asks ChatGPT “who's the best SEO agency in Galway?” or Perplexity “best Shopify developer in Ireland”, the model doesn't rank ten blue links. It composes an answer from the sources it trusts — and names a handful of businesses.

The rules are different from classic SEO. AI models favour clear, factual, well-structured content, consistent business information across the web, independent citations and directory listings, and schema that leaves no doubt about who you are and what you do. We optimise for all of it.

Common questions

Answered honestly

What is AI search optimisation (GEO)?+

Generative Engine Optimisation is the practice of making your business visible in answers from AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews. It combines structured data, consistent business citations, answer-formatted content and crawler access so AI systems can find, trust and recommend you.

Is GEO replacing SEO?+

No — it's the next layer on top of it. Google still drives most buying journeys, but AI assistants are the fastest-growing way people research purchases. The businesses named in AI answers tend to be the ones with strong SEO fundamentals plus clean, consistent entity data. We do both.

How long until my business shows up in AI answers?+

AI systems update at different speeds — some pull live web results, others change slowly. Most clients see measurable movement in AI answers within 2–4 months of consistent entity, citation and content work.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend me?+

No honest agency can guarantee what a third-party AI will say. What we guarantee is the work: correct structured data, broad citation coverage, answer-ready content and monthly tracking — the inputs that demonstrably influence AI visibility.
